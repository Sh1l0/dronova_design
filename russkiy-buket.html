Russian Bouquet

A flower delivery company operating worldwide, with an online store and dedicated iOS and Android apps. The company also has localized international domains, including a presence in Germany.


For this company, I worked extensively on the brand identity, as well as the UX/UI design of its website and mobile applications.

Site IOS Android Branding

Advertising & Outdoor Materials

Targeted advertising campaigns were launched for the company across Yandex and VK, helping to significantly attract new audiences and customers. I also created layouts for outdoor advertising, including a dynamic banner displayed on large digital screens across the city.

Collaboration Design

The company partners with external services to expand its loyal audience and create additional value for customers. For these collaborations, I designed banners, gift cards, and website reminders highlighting the option to split payments through partner services.

RB x Digift

Office Design

As part of the new office opening, I was responsible for adapting and coordinating the office design in line with the brand’s visual concept, which I had previously developed.

New Website & App Pages

As a UX/UI designer, I introduced new features across the website and mobile applications, developing their logic, structure, and visual concepts to ensure a clear and seamless user experience.

Adding New Features

To improve user convenience and expand the company’s range of services, I worked together with the marketing team to introduce the option of adding complementary products to an order. This feature not only enhanced the customer experience but also directly contributed to increased company revenue.

Loyalty Program Interface

As part of the strategy to retain new users and increase conversion, a loyalty program was introduced. The design concept is built around interactivity: the flower grows and develops as the customer progresses through different loyalty levels, creating a simple and visual way to track their status.

Email Validation Logic

As the business grew and the number of customers and user accounts increased, there was a need to develop a clear logic for email validation.

I designed and structured this logic and presented it as a visual flowchart to make the process clear and easy to implement.
