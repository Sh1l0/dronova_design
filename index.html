Web Design & Landings

I design websites and landing pages based on an agreed visual concept. I have professional expertise in Figma, Tilda, Adobe and AI tools, which allows me to implement projects quickly and efficiently.

Logotype

A logo is often the first introduction to a brand. It should be recognizable, distinctive, and clearly communicate the company’s values and ideas. My knowledge of brand building allows me to create logos that carry meaning and reflect the essence of a brand.

Visual Identity & Brand Guidelines

A distinctive brand personality comes from carefully developing each visual element. Together, they form a cohesive visual identity and build brand recognition. A clear set of guidelines helps maintain a consistent style across every touchpoint.

Social Media

Social media is a point of quick interaction with a brand and a place where people are constantly reminded of it. Posts need to be understood within seconds — instantly communicating which brand they belong to in an endless feed, what product is being offered, and how to get it. These are exactly the goals I focus on when designing for social media.
